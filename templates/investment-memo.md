# Investment committee research memo

**As of / instrument / mandate / research status / reviewer:**

## Financial conclusion
Valuation, priced-in assumptions, substantiated variant view, scenarios, expected-return assumptions and downside. Keep assumptions distinct from facts.

## Sustainability and impact conclusion
Label/transition/nature/social integrity, additionality, verification and adverse effects. Do not conflate this conclusion with financial attractiveness.

## Instrument and implementation
Exact security or private contract; candidate status; entry condition; liquidity and quote evidence; hedging, funding, legal and portfolio constraints. A programme without an investable instrument must be labelled accordingly.

## Catalyst and falsification
Dated evidence, monitoring triggers, thesis breakers, strongest counterargument, review/expiry conditions and decisions that would change with missing evidence.

## Evidence, calculations and unresolved issues
Cite stage/claim IDs, source locators, calculation IDs and methodology versions. Preserve material gaps and contradictions in the executive conclusion.

## Human research review
Actual accountable reviewer and rationale. No execution authority. No claim of independent audit certification or guaranteed suitability.
