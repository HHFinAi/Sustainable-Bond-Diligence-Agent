# Trade-aware research expression — not an order

Identify a specific security/contract or state NO_INVESTMENT_ROUTE. Record the valuation basis, entry condition, catalyst, downside, liquidity, hedging, thesis breakers and portfolio constraints. Supply source-backed quote dates or private contract evidence, and classify each eligibility check PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN.

The default is RESEARCH_ONLY. PUBLIC_MARKET_CANDIDATE requires fresh instrument-level quote evidence and all declared checks passing; PRIVATE_MARKET_CANDIDATE requires reviewed contractual access and all declared checks passing. These checks do not prove actual suitability or liquidity. Human review and an independent execution process remain required. `execution_authorized` is always false.
