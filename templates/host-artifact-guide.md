# Host artifact guide

Use `examples/artifact-template.json` for a NEEDS_DATA envelope. Replace `run_id`, `input_digest`, `stage_id` and revision from the current packet; example IDs must not be reused. For COMPLETE, populate every stage-specific section in `agent.json`, at least one supported typed claim, the required standard reviews, and any required calculation or investment-expression contract.

FACT: cite reviewed subject evidence. A number copied from a source must bind `metric.evidence_id`, `metric.metric_id`, value and unit and match the claim period/scope. INFERENCE: cite the inputs and state assumptions and limitations. ASSUMPTION: give a reason, no invented source. METHOD: identify a registered standard and never treat a future/voluntary reference as a current legal rule. CALCULATION: refer to an allowlisted calculation record with provenance for each argument, explicit units and a recomputable result.

Use unique IDs within each artifact. Evidence IDs refer to the frozen request; claim IDs are scoped to the stage and cited as stage/claim in exports. Every material issue must be carried into the issue register with severity and status. The engine validates fields and calculations, not that the section prose is scientifically, financially or legally sound. Human source-entailment review remains essential.
